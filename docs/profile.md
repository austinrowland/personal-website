## 3. Content pillars

1. **Agents are the new audience.** Anchor: his authored Postman post
   (July 2026). Agents don't read docs like people, don't open tickets
   when stuck, and silently switch to a competitor API when blocked. The
   AI-Ready APIs assessment: 25 deterministic checks, 5 categories (API
   Design, Developer Experience, Agent Discovery, Agent Understanding,
   Agent Usability), 7 surfaces; two tiers (API Product Foundations +
   Agent Journey Optimizations); grades from Agent-Optimized down to Not
   AI-Ready; built on existing standards (OpenAPI, MCP, OAuth, RFC 9457,
   llms.txt).
2. **Field work over prophecy.** "Go and watch where it fails. Then
   improve the route." (YesPress). His role converts abstract executive
   anxiety into a sequence of inspectable failures and a repair list.
3. **Build, Operate, Scale.** His FDE team's model, in his own words: work
   with a strategic enterprise to validate a novel problem, build the
   solution, validate across more customers, then scale learnings back
   into product and playbooks. Proof point: ~400 sellers trained on his
   team's pitch playbook at Sales Kick-Off; two of his customers featured
   in the CEO keynote.
4. **Velocity.** "Orchestrate 3, 5, or 10 workflows at the same time."
   Imperfect demos over perfect presentations — more reps raise the
   batting average. Job security as an engineer: producing faster, better
   output with these tools than they could produce alone.
5. **Ambition balanced with discipline.** TEDx 2023: ambition expands the
   field of possibility; discipline moves you across it. Gratitude as a
   way to recharge. "You can do anything, but you can't do everything."
   The corrective question: "Am I becoming the person I want to become?"
6. **Scope is not surrender.** The 5,000-cookie ambition negotiated down
   to 500 — the retreat saved the idea, the first 500 created evidence,
   evidence attracted helpers. YesPress names it as an engineer's lesson;
   the same pattern runs through Avova, TOM, and how he runs projects.
7. **Health and accessibility roots.** Stroke rehab gloves with CV-based
   recovery tracking; Avova Health (preventative health, 100+ patient
   interviews); TOM projects — eye-gaze communication software, custom
   wheelchairs, a piano pedal bite switch. Where "user" means "person"
   most literally.
8. **Security roots.** GTRI critical infrastructure → Cisco cloud
   security → Palo Alto Networks firewall OS and VPN daemon. The agents
   work stands on real infrastructure and security depth, not prompt
   enthusiasm.
