# Writing covers

Every piece in `src/content/writing/` gets a generated cover in one style: a
Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print that matches the J2 logo and the Story ink
drawings. Generate a 1536×1024 image, save it as
`src/assets/writing/<slug>.jpg` (mozjpeg, quality ~82), and set `cover` and
`coverAlt` in the post's frontmatter.

## Prompt

Keep this block as is and change only the subject line:

> Editorial cover illustration for an essay titled "<title>", about <one-line
> theme>. Style: Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print, flat colors with bold black
> ink outlines, limited palette of warm off-white paper (#F6F3EE), ink black,
> soft grey, a pale sun disc, and one accent of burnt orange. Subject:
> <a single calm scene that stands for the essay's idea>. Large calm negative
> space, subtle paper and woodgrain texture, no text, no letters, no seals, no
> logos. Wide landscape framing.

## Subjects so far

- **Ambition Might Be Your Achilles Heel:** a lone figure at the base of a
  steep mountain path that zigzags up to a summit, small wave arcs at the
  bottom.
- **AI-Ready APIs:** a torii gateway on a small island in calm water, with
  paper boats carrying small orange lights drifting toward the gate, distant
  mountains, seigaiha waves at the bottom.
